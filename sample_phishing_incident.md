Day 25: Basic Incident Response

Sample Scenario: Phishing Email Incident

1. Incident Description

An employee receives a suspicious email asking them to click a link and sign in to their account. The employee clicks the link and reports the email to the security team.

2. Detection and Analysis

The security team checks the sender's email address, message details, and available sign-in logs to determine whether the account may be compromised.

3. Containment

The team quarantines the suspicious email and applies appropriate account protection measures if credential exposure is suspected.

4. Eradication

The team removes related malicious emails and addresses any confirmed account compromise or security weaknesses.

5. Recovery

The team verifies secure account access and monitors activity for further suspicious behavior.

6. Lessons Learned

The team documents the incident, reviews the response, and recommends better email filtering and phishing awareness training.
